## 2. Executive summary

Day la mot paper applied micro rat "sach" va rat dang doc vi no dat mot cau hoi vua pho bien vua bi bo qua: khi nguoi truong thanh mat di mot cha me, tac dong lao dong va thu nhap cua ho keo dai den dau, va thong qua co che nao? Thay vi chi dung o phuc loi tam ly, paper dua cau hoi vao mot khung labor economics ro rang: parental death co de lai mot labor market penalty dai han hay khong.

Tac gia dung du lieu hanh chinh toan dan cua Dan Mach, tap trung vao **sudden first parental deaths** trong giai doan 1985-2014 doi voi con truong thanh 25-50 tuoi, roi ghep tung nguoi bi tac dong voi mot matched control co dac diem quan sat tuong tu va uoc luong mot matched-control difference-in-differences / event-study. Y tuong nhan dang cot loi la neu cai chet den dot ngot, thi kha nang co anticipatory adjustment thap hon; neu chi xet **first parental death**, thi kenh thua ke hay dieu chinh quanh second death bi lam yeu di.

Ket qua chinh rat ro: sau 5 nam, thu nhap cua con trai giam khoang **2%**, con thu nhap cua con gai giam khoang **3%** so voi matched controls. Cai chet cua **me** gay tac dong xau hon cai chet cua **cha** cho ca con trai lan con gai, va tac dong cua parental death lon hon voi **phu nu**. Khi di sau vao co che, paper cho thay suc khoe tam than xau di ro ret sau parental death, va phu nu co con nho mat thu nhap manh hon nhieu, phu hop voi kenh mat di informal childcare tu ong ba. Kenh eldercare cho parent con song co xuat hien, nhung co ve khong phai co che chinh.

Bai nay dang doc vi no lam duoc ba viec cung luc: dat cau hoi quan trong, dung mot design rat hoc-thuat-ma-gon, va dua ra mot cau chuyen co che kha thuyet phuc. No bien bereavement thanh mot labor-market shock co the dinh luong duoc, tu do mo ra mot agenda policy va research rat ro.

## 3. Research question and motivation

### Cau hoi nghien cuu trung tam

Parental death trong tuoi truong thanh anh huong the nao den employment va earnings cua con, va nhung tac dong do di qua kenh nao?

### Dong luc hoc thuat va thuc tien

Phan lon literature ve family health shocks tap trung vao cai chet hoac benh nang cua vo/chong, hoac cua con cai. Do la nhung cu soc rat nghiem trong, nhung hiem hoi hon va kho tong quat hoa ra toan bo dan so trong do tuoi lao dong. Parental death thi khac: day la mot bien co gan nhu pho quat. Chinh vi pho quat, ngay ca mot penalty thu nhap khong qua lon o cap ca nhan van co the co y nghia aggregate rat dang ke.

Co it nhat bon co che hop ly: grief va xau di suc khoe tam than; mat informal childcare tu ong ba; tang nghia vu cham soc nguoi cha/me con song; va thay doi uu tien giua cong viec va gia dinh sau bien co lon. Khoang trong ma paper lap la o cho literature chua co mot empirical paper that chat nao noi ro parental death khi con da la nguoi lon se di vao labor market nhu the nao va qua kenh gi.

## 4. Main contribution

### Dong gop chinh so voi van lieu truoc

Dong gop lon nhat cua paper la chi ra rang parental death khong chi la mot bien co tam ly hay gia dinh, ma la mot **labor market shock co y nghia kinh te** va keo dai nhieu nam. Diem nay thay doi framing: tu "bereavement" sang "earnings and employment consequences of family loss".

### Diem moi ve du lieu va thiet ke

- Dung du lieu register toan dan cua Dan Mach, cho phep lien ket child-parent, earnings, employment, thuoc men, psychologist visits, hospital contacts, cau truc gia dinh va vi tri song.
- Gioi han mau vao **first parental death** va **sudden deaths** de lam ro logic nhan dang va tach cac co che.
- Dung nearest-neighbor matching ket hop event-study / DiD thay vi chi so sanh before-after thuan tuy.
- Kiem tra duoc mot loat co che rat cu the, khong chi dung lai o reduced-form earnings effect.

### Vi sao dong gop nay quan trong trong applied micro

Paper rat "applied micro" theo nghia dep nhat: mot cau hoi doi thuong nhung quan trong, du lieu hanh chinh rat manh, strategy nhan dang minh bach, outcome y nghia kinh te ro, va co che duoc mo ra bang cac bien trung gian do duoc. No cung noi dep labor, family, gender va health economics vao cung mot bai.

## 5. Identification or methodology

### Du lieu va mau phan tich

Tac gia dung du lieu population registers cua Dan Mach, theo doi dan so tu 1980 den 2019. Mau treatment gom **tat ca sudden first parental deaths xay ra trong 1985-2014** cho nhung nguoi con **25-50 tuoi** co hai cha me duoc nhan dien trong du lieu. Table 1 cho thay co **206,286 sudden first parental deaths** trong mau.

### Vi sao chi xet sudden first parental death?

- **Sudden death** giup giam lo ngai anticipatory effects. Neu parent benh lau ngay, con co the da dieu chinh gio lam, cho o hay vai tro cham soc truoc khi death xay ra.
- **First parental death** giup han che vai tro cua inheritance va cac dieu chinh lien quan den second death. Trong boi canh Dan Mach, bequest thuong quan trong hon sau second death.

Day la mot lua chon rat thong minh: tac gia khong co gang nhan dang tat ca cac parental deaths, ma tap trung vao phan shock "sach" nhat de lay internal validity.

### Matching strategy

Moi treated individual duoc ghep voi mot control khong mat bat ky parent nao trong vong 5 nam sau thoi diem treated mat parent. Tac gia:

- exact-match theo gender, nhom earnings, va viec co con nho duoi 6 tuoi hay khong tai nam truoc bien co
- nearest-neighbor match theo Mahalanobis distance trong tung exact-match cell
- dung them age, education, public/private sector, cohabitation, region, so con, tuoi cha me, tuoi con nho nhat, employment va earnings history 3 nam truoc shock

Nhin theo ngon ngu applied micro, ho dang co gang tao ra mot "counterfactual labor trajectory" rat gan cho tung nguoi bi tac dong.

### Event study va DiD

Sau khi matching, tac gia uoc luong:

- event study trong cua so 5 nam truoc va 5 nam sau parental death
- DiD tong hop de lay average post effect
- specification tach rieng tac dong cua mother death va father death

Ho dua vao year fixed effects, individual-by-match fixed effects, event-time fixed effects, age controls, va cluster standard errors theo individual-by-match ID. Logic nhan dang trung tam la: neu khong co parental death, treated va matched control se co quỹ dao earnings/employment giong nhau quanh bien co.

### Diem manh cua phuong phap

- Design de giai thich va giam sat bang event study rat ro.
- Matching lam bai toan so sanh giua nam va nu, giua nguoi co con nho va khong co con nho, tro nen hop ly hon.
- Viec tranh "already-treated controls" giup bai ne duoc mot so van de quen thuoc cua staggered DiD.

### Diem de bi nghi ngo

- Sudden death van co the khong hoan toan ngau nhien. Mot so health risks cua gia dinh co the van lien quan toi labor trajectories cua con.
- Parallel trends chu yeu duoc bao ve bang matching va event-study visuals, khong phai bang mot natural experiment "sach hon" nhu IV hay RD.
- Dan Mach la boi canh co childcare, eldercare va healthcare cong kha manh, nen external validity can duoc doc can than.

Noi ngan gon: identification cua paper khong phai "bulletproof", nhung la dang rat thuyet phuc trong khung du lieu hanh chinh va cau hoi nghien cuu nay.

## 6. Results and interpretation

### Ket qua tong the

Event study cho thay truoc parental death, treated va matched controls co xu huong employment va earnings gan nhu trung nhau. Sau shock, ca hai gioi deu giam employment va earnings, nhung phu nu bi anh huong manh hon.

Den nam thu 5 sau parental death:

- employment giam khoang **0.5 pp** doi voi nam va **1 pp** doi voi nu
- tong labor supply giam khoang **1.2%** doi voi nam va **1.7%** doi voi nu
- earnings giam khoang **7,000 DKK**
- ty le giam earnings tuong doi la **2%** voi nam va **3%** voi nu

Day la mot ket qua dep o cho no vua co y nghia thong ke vua de doc ve mat kinh te. Giam 2-3% nghe co ve khong "soc", nhung vi bien co nay rat pho bien, aggregate burden cua no la dang ke.

### Mother death vs. father death

Paper cho thay cai chet cua **me** gay tac dong xau hon cai chet cua **cha** cho ca con trai lan con gai. Dong thoi, cung mot parental death, **con gai** mat thu nhap nhieu hon con trai.

Diem nay quan trong vi no bat dau goi y co che. Neu day chi la grief theo nghia chung chung, co the khac biet giua mother va father se yeu hon. Nhung khi tac dong cua mother death lon hon, ta nghi ngay toi vai tro cua me trong childcare support va emotional infrastructure cua gia dinh.

### Mental health channel

Day la phan bang chung co che thuyet phuc nhat cua bai. Sau parental death:

- phu nu tang psychologist visits **0.10** lan sau mother death va **0.06** lan sau father death
- nam tang psychologist visits **0.031** lan sau mother death va **0.018** lan sau father death
- mental-health prescriptions tang **1.5 pp** va **0.9 pp** cho nu sau mother/father death
- mental-health prescriptions tang **1.0 pp** va **0.6 pp** cho nam
- opioid prescriptions cung tang, khoang **5%** doi voi nu va **6%** doi voi nam so voi baseline

Khong can phai cho thay mot "clinical crisis" moi duoc coi la co che. O day, chi can thay dieu tri tam ly va su dung thuoc tang dung luc earnings di xuong, la cau chuyen da rat manh: grief khong chi la emotional cost ma con di vao productivity va labor attachment.

### Informal childcare channel

Day la phan giup paper that su dang nho. Tac gia chia mau theo viec co con duoi 6 tuoi hay khong. Ket qua:

- voi nhung nguoi **khong** co con nho, nam va nu co muc giam earnings kha gan nhau
- voi phu nu **co con nho**, earnings giam gan **4%** sau mother death va khoang **3.5%** sau father death
- voi nam co con nho, muc giam chi quanh **1%**

Day la bang chung rat hop logic cho kenh mat informal childcare. Ong ba, dac biet la ba ngoai/noi, la mot phan cua ha tang cham soc tre khong chinh thuc. Khi mat ho, phu nu la nguoi thuong "nhan" shock lao dong lon hon. Paper co them mot manh ghep hay: formal childcare uptake co tang, nhung khong du de bu het mat mat tu informal childcare.

### Eldercare channel

Tac gia cung xem xet viec parental death co the day con vao vai tro cham soc nguoi cha/me con song, dac biet khi nguoi con lai suc khoe kem. Co mot vai bang chung goi y rang phu nu bi anh huong hon trong truong hop nay, va nu cung co xu huong o gan widowed parent hon sau shock. Tuy nhien, chinh paper ket luan kenh nay co le **khong phai driver chinh** cua general earnings decline.

Day la diem dep trong cach viet bai: ho khong co gang "ban" moi co che nhu nhau. Ho phan cap muc do tin cay cua tung kenh.

### Cac phan robustness va bo sung

Paper bao cao khong co bang chung cho thay ket qua chu yeu di qua:

- bequest channel
- thay doi fertility hay cohabitation
- doi occupation hay doi firm do thay doi preferences

Tac dong manh hon khi parent-con song gan nhau, khi parent mat tre hon, khi con tre hon luc shock, va trong cac giai doan gan day hon. Ket qua cung robust khi doi control group sang nhung nguoi soon-to-be-treated.

## 7. What is special or elegant about the paper

Co ba diem lam bai nay dep:

### Mot shock rat doi thuong, mot design rat nghiem

Nhieu paper hay co cau hoi lon nhung shock hiem, hoac shock ro nhung cau hoi hep. Bai nay lam duoc dieu kho hon: lay mot bien co gan nhu pho quat va dinh luong no that nghiem tuc.

### Co che duoc mo bang "chi tiet dung cho cau hoi"

Paper khong lan sang qua nhieu outcomes. Ho chon dung nhung do luong phuc vu logic bai:

- psychologist visits va prescriptions cho mental health
- young children cho childcare
- surviving parent health cho eldercare

Moi bien trung gian deu gan chat voi co che ma paper muon kiem.

### Bai hoc ve gender economics rat tu nhien

Paper khong bat dau bang "gender paper" mot cach len gong, nhung ket qua cuoi cung rat manh ve gender. Day la kieu bai rat dang hoc: bat dau tu mot shock chung, roi de du lieu tu nhien mo ra bat binh dang gioi.

## 8. Required background knowledge

De doc paper nay muot, nguoi doc nen nam bon cum nen tang:

### Matched DiD / event study

Can hieu DiD la so sanh thay doi truoc-sau giua nhom bi tac dong va nhom doi chung. O day, matching duoc dung truoc de lam cho doi chung "giong" hon.

### Parallel trends

Gia dinh cot loi la neu khong co parental death, treated va matched controls se di cung mot xu huong ve earnings/employment. Day la phan can doc ky tren do thi pre-trends.

### Extensive margin va intensive margin

- **Extensive margin:** co di lam hay khong
- **Intensive margin:** da di lam thi lam nhieu hay it, thu nhap/cuong do lao dong ra sao

Paper tach duoc hai margin nay nen cho phep doc tac dong dep hon.

### Informal childcare va family support

Trong family economics, ong ba co the duoc hieu nhu mot input cua labor supply cua con cai, dac biet la phu nu co con nho. Mat input nay co the day chi phi di lam len cao hon.

### Bereavement va labor economics

Can quen voi y tuong rang suc khoe tam than, stress, grief va prescription use khong chi la outcomes y te; chung con anh huong truc tiep den productivity, labor supply va earnings.

## 9. Limitations and open questions

### Han che chinh

Han che lon nhat la identification van dua tren conditional comparability hon la mot natural experiment cuc ky sach. Matching rat tot, nhung van khong the loai bo het unobservables.

### External validity

Dan Mach co he thong childcare, eldercare va healthcare cong tuong doi manh. Chinh paper lap luan rang ket qua o nhung noi he thong yeu hon co the con lon hon, nhung day la suy luan hop ly, chua phai bang chung truc tiep.

### Do luong co che

Mental health duoc do kha tot, nhung co che preference change, grief intensity, hay phan bo lai thoi gian trong ho gia dinh van chu yeu la suy dien tu cac outcome gian tiep.

### Cac cau hoi mo

- Tac dong co thay doi theo social class, immigrant background, hay family structure khong?
- Vai tro cua sibling network la gi? Co nhung gia dinh "phan bo" grief va care responsibilities tot hon gia dinh khac hay khong?
- Cac chinh sach bereavement leave hay counseling co lam giam earnings penalty nay khong?

## 10. Takeaways for a researcher

- Chon mot shock rat pho bien co the tao ra dong gop lon, neu minh do duoc outcome va co che that chat.
- Matching truoc khi lam event-study la mot cach rat huu ich khi so sanh giua cac nhom co quỹ dao song va lao dong khac nhau.
- Neu muon thuyet phuc ve co che, hay chon outcomes trung gian thuc su "di qua" logic bai, thay vi thu qua nhieu thu.
- Khac biet gioi tinh thuong hien ra manh hon khi design cua bai cho phep gan ket shock voi ha tang cham soc trong gia dinh.
- Mot reduced-form effect 2-3% van rat lon neu bien co co prevalence cao.
- Bai viet ket luan rat hay: tu mot uoc luong vi mo chuyen sang ham y policy ma khong bao gio di qua xa khoi evidence.

## 11. Vietnam relevance and extension

### Paper nay lien quan den Viet Nam o dau?

Rat lien quan. O Viet Nam, informal childcare cua ong ba va mang luoi ho tro gia dinh con co the quan trong hon Dan Mach, trong khi he thong grief counseling, bereavement support hay paid leave cho con cai khi mat cha me gan nhu khong duoc thiet ke ro rang. Neu vay, neu co mot earnings penalty sau parental death, kha nang cao la no khong nho.

### Co the trien khai y tuong nghien cuu nay o Viet Nam khong?

Co, nhung se kho hon o du lieu. De lam that tot, can it nhat mot trong cac nguon sau:

- du lieu hanh chinh co lien ket parent-child va thong tin tu vong
- du lieu BHXH / payroll / thue cho earnings, employment
- du lieu y te hoac BHYT cho prescription, kham chua benh
- neu khong co admin linked data, can panel survey manh va bo sung module ve grief, childcare, caregiving

### Tro ngai chinh

- Lien ket parent-child trong du lieu hanh chinh Viet Nam co the chua sac net hoac kho tiep can
- Earnings trong khu vuc phi chinh thuc rat kho do
- Mental health treatment bi under-measured vi stigma va tiep can dich vu thap
- Informal childcare pho bien hon, nen can phan biet mat ho tro cua ong ba voi nhung family shocks khac

### Cach mo rong cho Viet Nam

Huong hop ly la dung shock parental death de nghien cuu labor supply cua phu nu co con nho, nhat la trong khu vuc cong nhan, khu cong nghiep, hoac lao dong di cu noi dia. O day, informal childcare cua ong ba co the la mot "ha tang an" rat quan trong nhung it khi xuat hien trong du lieu chinh sach.

### Mot so research question / design co the phat trien

1. **Parental death va labor supply cua lao dong nu co con nho tai Viet Nam**
   Su dung du lieu hanh chinh neu co, hoac mot employer-linked panel trong cac khu cong nghiep lon.

2. **Grandparent childcare va kha nang giu viec cua lao dong di cu**
   Kiem tra xem mat di mot ong/ba co anh huong manh hon voi lao dong di cu khong co mang ho tro dia phuong hay khong.

3. **Bereavement shocks va suc khoe tam than / nang suat**
   Ket hop survey voi employer records hoac clinical screening nho de xem grief co di vao absenteeism, presenteeism, va job turnover nhu the nao.

Noi ngan gon, bai nay mo ra mot huong nghien cuu rat phu hop voi Viet Nam, dac biet o giao diem giua labor, gender, social protection va family support systems.
