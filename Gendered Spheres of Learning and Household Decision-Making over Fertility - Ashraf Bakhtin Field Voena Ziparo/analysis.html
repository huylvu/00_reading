2. Executive summary

Paper hoi mot cau rat hay va rat applied: khi vo chong cung quyet dinh sinh con nhung chi nguoi vo truc tiep trai qua mang thai va sinh no, lieu bat can xung thong tin ve rui ro suc khoe sinh san co the tu no tao ra bat dong ve muc sinh hay khong. Bai tra loi bang cach ket hop mot mo hinh ly thuyet ve strategic communication trong ho gia dinh voi mot field experiment o Zambia, noi tac gia ngau nhien hoa viec nguoi chong hay nguoi vo duoc nhan thong tin ve rui ro suc khoe ba me.

Ket qua cot loi la: cung cap thong tin cho ca hai phia deu lam giam xac suat mang thai trong nam tiep theo, nhung co che va he qua phuc loi rat khac nhau. Khi nguoi chong duoc cung cap thong tin, anh ta cap nhat niem tin, giam nhu cau co them con, truyen thong tin sang vo, va muc sinh giam ma khong keo theo giam chuyen giao cho vo. Khi nguoi vo duoc cung cap thong tin, co ay cap nhat niem tin nhung khong truyen duoc sang chong, nen muc sinh cung giam nhung di kem giam qua tang, ho tro va mot so chi bao well-being cua nguoi vo. Y tuong lon cua bai la: thong tin khong tu dong "chay" trong ho gia dinh; khi loi ich xung dot, nguoi co thong tin chua chac truyen duoc no mot cach dang tin.

Day la paper rat dang doc vi no nang mot truc giac quen thuoc trong development economics, rang thong tin quan trong, thanh mot phat hien sac hon: dieu quan trong khong chi la ai biet gi, ma la ai duoc nghe truc tiep, ai kiem soat transfer, va ai co kha nang phat tin hieu dang tin trong mot quan he co bargaining.

3. Research question and motivation

Cau hoi trung tam

Lieu chenh lech thong tin giua vo va chong ve rui ro suc khoe ba me co phai la mot nguon tao ra chenh lech ve desired fertility va realized fertility hay khong? Va neu co, tai sao thong tin ma nguoi vo co lai khong tu nhien lan sang nguoi chong de giai quyet bat dong?

Dong luc hoc thuat

Van lieu ve fertility va household bargaining tu lau da nhan manh rang vo chong co the co preferred fertility khac nhau. Nhung phan lon mo hinh ho gia dinh hoac ngam gia dinh thong tin chung, hoac it nhat gia dinh thong tin moi co the duoc truyen tai tuong doi tron tru trong gia dinh. Paper nay dat van de nguoc lai: neu chi phi sinh con chu yeu do phu nu truc tiep ganh chiu, thi phu nu co the hoc duoc nhieu hon ve risk; tuy nhien chinh vi preferences giua hai ben khong trung nhau, truyen dat thong tin do co the khong credible.

Dong luc thuc tien

Trong boi canh Zambia, maternal health risk con cao, fertility cao, va nam gioi thuong it hien dien trong cac khong gian kham thai hoac sinh no. Dieu do tao ra mot setting rat hop de nghien cuu "gendered spheres of learning": phu nu biet nhieu hon vi truc tiep trai nghiem va tiep xuc voi he thong y te, con nam gioi ra quyet dinh mot phan ve fertility nhung lai it biet hon ve cost thuc su cua childbearing.

4. Main contribution

Dong gop chinh so voi van lieu truoc

Dong gop quan trong nhat cua paper la chi ra rang chenh lech ve thong tin doi voi chi phi sinh con la mot nguon doc lap cua chenh lech ve desired fertility giua nam va nu. Day khong chi la cau chuyen "nam muon nhieu con hon", ma la "nam muon nhieu con hon mot phan vi ho biet it hon ve maternal risk".

Diem moi ve du lieu va thiet ke

  • Tac gia co du lieu rat hiem ve beliefs cua ca hai vo chong lien quan den maternal mortality risk.
  • Ho ngau nhien hoa nguoi nhan thong tin trong cap doi, nho do tach duoc direct effect khoi within-household spillover.
  • Ho khong dung o fertility outcomes ma con nhin vao transfers, emotional support, marital satisfaction va sex-life satisfaction de doc bargaining consequences.

Diem moi ve ly thuyet

Paper dua logic Crawford-Sobel style strategic communication vao mot bai toan fertility va household bargaining. Day la phan rat dep cua bai: thong tin khong lan tu vo sang chong khong phai vi phu nu khong biet noi, ma vi chong co ly do de discount thong tin do vo cung cap khi anh ta tin rang vo co dong co chien luoc de day fertility xuong.

Vi sao contribution nay quan trong

Bai nay nam rat dung giao diem giua development, family economics va applied micro. No thay doi cach ta nghi ve interventions trong reproductive health: khong the chi gia dinh rang "day phu nu roi phu nu se ve noi lai voi chong". Neu communication bi rang buoc boi strategic conflict, targeting information sai nguoi co the lam giam fertility nhung dong thoi lam xau di welfare noi ho.

5. Identification or methodology

Thiet ke thuc nghiem

Tac gia trien khai mot field experiment voi cac cap doi thu nhap thap o Zambia trong boi canh workshop ve suc khoe sinh san. Moi nguoi deu duoc tiep can family planning information; treatment la them mot module ve maternal mortality va morbidity risk. Diem ngau nhien hoa mau chot la module nay duoc dua cho nguoi chong hoac nguoi vo, cho phep so sanh:

  • direct effect len nguoi duoc nhan thong tin
  • spillover effect len nguoi phoi ngau khong truc tiep duoc nhan
  • household-level effect tuy theo ai la nguoi duoc treatment

Thiet ke nay rat hay vi no tranh nham lan giua "tac dung cua noi dung thong tin" va "tac dung cua viec chi rieng mot nguoi trong household biet thong tin".

Bien ket qua chinh

  • Risk-awareness index ve maternal health risk
  • Beliefs ve likelihood of complications
  • Fertility goals / desired fertility
  • Pregnancy trong nam tiep theo
  • Transfer outcomes: co qua hay khong, gia tri qua, emotional support
  • Well-being outcomes: marital happiness, sex-life satisfaction

Logic nhan dang

Nhan dang dua tren random assignment cua nguoi nhan thong tin trong household. Vi cung mot noi dung thong tin nhung khac nguoi nhan, chenh lech outcome giua arm "husband treated" va arm "wife treated" duoc dien giai nhu bang chung ve asymmetry in information transmission va bargaining consequences, thay vi chi la tac dong trung binh cua information campaign.

Mo hinh ly thuyet

Mo hinh gia dinh hai thu cung ton tai:

  1. Vo va chong co preferred fertility khac nhau.
  2. Chong co the dung transfers de dich chuyen fertility outcome ve phia minh.

Neu thong tin la symmetric, household co the tien gan efficient outcome thong qua transfers. Nhung khi chi vo duoc biet cost that, co ay co dong co chien luoc de "noi qua" risk nham giam fertility va tang transfer; biet dieu do, chong discount thong diep, nen communication fail. Nguoc lai, khi chong la nguoi duoc informed, truthful communication co the xay ra neu anh ta internalize du manh health cost cua vo va transfer khong qua dat.

Diem manh cua phuong phap

  • Randomized design rat clean cho cau hoi causal.
  • Ket hop theory va experiment that su chat, khong phai gan mo hinh cho dep.
  • Outcome set phong phu, cho phep phan biet fertility reduction "tot" voi fertility reduction "dat".

Diem de bi nghi ngo

  • Household outcomes nhu transfer hay marital satisfaction co thanh phan self-report.
  • Sample la cac cap doi dong y cung tham gia workshop nen co the cooperative hon dan so chung.
  • Time horizon mot nam du tot cho short-run fertility nhung chua tra loi fully ve long-run family size.

6. Results and interpretation

Baseline facts rat quan trong

Paper cho thay ngay tu baseline, nam gioi biet it hon phu nu ve maternal risk factors va thuong muon nhieu con hon. Trong sample, desired fertility trung binh cua nam la 4.43, cua nu la 4.19; 36.6% so cap co tinh huong chong muon nhieu con hon vo, con 35.7% la khong co gap. Day la bang chung nen de lap luan rang information asymmetry co the thuc su matter cho fertility bargaining.

Ket qua ve beliefs

Khi nguoi chong duoc cung cap thong tin, ca chong lan vo deu tang risk awareness. Tuc la information khong chi cap nhat beliefs cua nguoi nhan truc tiep ma con lan sang spouse. Nhung khi nguoi vo duoc cung cap thong tin, chi ban than nguoi vo cap nhat beliefs; chong hau nhu khong cap nhat. Day la bang chung gon va rat manh cho asymmetry in intra-household learning.

Ket qua ve fertility demand

Treated husbands bao cao lower demand for children. Trong khi do, husbands cua treated wives khong doi demand for children. Nghia la muon keo household ve muc fertility thap hon mot cach "em", can lam chong doi beliefs va preferences, chu khong chi lam vo so rui ro hon.

Ket qua ve realized fertility

Bang 5 cho thay ca hai arm deu lam giam fertility index. So voi control:

  • husband treated lam fertility index giam khoang 0.192 do lech chuan, xac suat vo dang mang thai giam khoang 5.3 diem phan tram, va reported likelihood co them con giam khoang 0.823 diem tren thang 0-10.
  • wife treated lam fertility index giam khoang 0.142 do lech chuan, xac suat dang mang thai giam khoang 4.1 diem phan tram, va likelihood co them con giam khoang 0.587 diem.

Diem tinh te la tac gia khong nhan rang husband treatment "giam fertility nhieu hon ro ret" theo nghia thong ke cho moi outcome; thay vao do, ho nhan rang hai arm deu giam fertility, nhung chi mot arm tranh duoc welfare cost cho phu nu.

Ket qua ve transfers va well-being

Day la phan dang nho nhat cua paper. Voi husband treated, transfer index gan nhu khong doi; xac suat co qua va gia tri qua khong giam, mot so chi bao quan he tham chi nhich len. Voi wife treated, transfer index giam khoang 0.161 do lech chuan; xac suat nhan qua va gia tri qua deu giam co y nghia thong ke. Hieu kinh te cua ket qua nay la:

  • Khi chi vo doi beliefs ma chong khong doi, giam fertility giong nhu mot outcome ma vo phai "tra gia" bang bargaining.
  • Khi chong cung doi beliefs, household giam fertility theo huong gan joint optimum hon, nen khong can reallocate transfers bat loi cho vo.

Heterogeneity dang chu y

Tac gia con cho thay hieu ung manh hon trong nhung cap ma maternal risk cua vo co ve cao hon, nhu vo lon tuoi hon hoac co tien su birth complications. Dieu nay lam cau chuyen co che hop logic hon: neu thong tin thuc su la ve maternal risk, thi treatment nen manh hon dung o noi risk quan trong hon ve mat kinh te.

Y nghia rong hon

Bai nay khong chi noi "information works". No noi:

  • information works through bargaining
  • the same information can create different welfare consequences depending on who receives it
  • fertility reduction khong tu dong dong nghia voi welfare improvement

7. What is special or elegant about the paper

Diem dep nhat cua paper la no noi ba thu thuong bi nghien cuu tach roi:

  1. Household bargaining
  2. Information transmission
  3. Reproductive health and fertility behavior

Rat nhieu paper chi dung o viec chung minh mot intervention doi outcome. Bai nay di xa hon: no giai thich vi sao cung mot intervention nhung delivered to husband va delivered to wife lai cho household equilibrium khac nhau.

Diem dep thu hai la cach tac gia dung mot mo hinh strategic communication rat co dien nhung dat no vao mot setting cuc doi thuong va policy-relevant. Nho vay, theory khong bi treo lo lung ma doc xong thay "a, dung la trong household, nguoi co thong tin chua chac thuyet phuc duoc nguoi con lai neu incentives khong dong huong".

Diem dep thu ba la paper khong romanticize "empowering women through information" theo cach qua don gian. No chi ra mot su that kho chiu nhung huu ich cho policy: neu cau truc bargaining khong doi, them information cho phu nu co the cai thien mot so outcome nhung dong thoi lam ho phai chiu cost lon hon trong household.

8. Required background knowledge

Household bargaining

Nguoi doc nen biet cac mo hinh household bargaining, dac biet la y tuong rang fertility khong phai luc nao cung la mot quyet dinh cua "ho gia dinh nhu mot nguoi duy nhat". Vo va chong co the co utility rieng, outside options rieng, va quyen kiem soat transfer khac nhau.

Strategic communication / cheap talk

Can co truc giac co ban ve Crawford-Sobel. Khi sender va receiver co loi ich khac nhau, sender co the co dong co bop meo thong tin; receiver biet dieu do nen khong tin hoan toan. Ket qua la communication co the pooling hoac chi partially informative.

Treatment spillovers within household

Trong bai nay, spillover khong phai externality kieu lang xom ma la information spillover trong cap doi. Day la diem rat applied nhung cuc quan trong: do treatment effect cho nguoi nhan truc tiep la chua du neu research question la household decision-making.

Fertility economics trong boi canh dang phat trien

Nguoi doc nen co nen ve unmet need for family planning, ideal fertility, birth spacing, va viec male demand for children thuong la mot nhan to quan trong trong sub-Saharan Africa. Neu khong co nen nay, se kho thay vi sao mot can thiep thong tin nho lai dang chu y den vay.

Welfare interpretation

Paper buoc ta phan biet outcome reduction voi welfare improvement. Cung la fertility giam, nhung neu di kem giam transfers va support cho phu nu, thi do khong con la mot chien thang policy don gian nua.

9. Limitations and open questions

Han che chinh

  1. Sample selection co the lam paper danh gia thap muc do communication failure trong dan so rong hon vi cac cap dong y cung tham gia workshop co the da tuong doi hop tac.
  2. Mot phan outcome la self-reported, nhat la communication, transfers va marital well-being.
  3. Horizon phan tich tuong doi ngan, chu yeu mot nam sau can thiep; chua biet long-run completed fertility, marital stability, hay contraceptive dynamics ra sao.
  4. Mo hinh household bargaining gia dinh cau truc transfer kha ro, trong khi thuc te bargaining co the phuc tap hon, gom bao luc, social norms, kin pressure, va repeated interaction.

Gia dinh nhay cam

  • Chong thuc su internalize mot phan chi phi suc khoe cua vo khi duoc thong tin.
  • Difference giua arm chong nhan va arm vo nhan chu yeu di qua information transmission va bargaining, chu khong phai chi vi attendance/attention khac nhau.
  • Transfer measures quan sat duoc du gan voi bargaining outcomes ma model nham toi.

Cau hoi mo

  • Lieu joint treatment cho ca hai cung luc co cho hieu ung con tot hon khong?
  • Tac dong co ben trong 2-5 nam khong, hay chi la short-run birth spacing?
  • Trong cac domain khac nhu child nutrition, schooling, vaccine, hay labor migration, co ton tai cung mot logic "gendered spheres of learning" khong?

10. Takeaways for a researcher

  1. Dung gia dinh information trong household tu dong shared; neu research question la household behavior, nguoi nhan thong tin la mot phan cua treatment.
  2. Neu outcome cuoi cung la household-level, hay do ca direct effect lan within-household spillover de doc co che.
  3. Policy evaluation nen nhin ca bargaining outcomes va welfare proxies, khong chi nhin behavioral endpoint nhu fertility hay take-up.
  4. Mot theoretical model ngan nhung that khop voi institutional setting co the nang han suc nang cua empirical design.
  5. Nhung bien "mem" nhu beliefs, perceived risk, hay self-reported agreement doi khi la mat xich thiet yeu de lam cho causal story thuyet phuc hon.
  6. Heterogeneity theo ex ante disagreement hoac ex ante risk rat quan trong khi paper noi ve communication failure; neu khong, mechanism de bi ke qua rong.

11. Vietnam relevance and extension

Lien he voi Viet Nam

Viet Nam khong co muc maternal risk giong Zambia, nhung logic nghien cuu van rat relevant. Trong nhieu quyet dinh ho gia dinh o Viet Nam, dac biet ve sinh con, sinh con thu hai/thu ba, khoang cach sinh, lua chon noi sinh, sang loc truoc sinh, va cham soc sau sinh, phu nu thuong tiep xuc truc tiep voi thong tin y te nhieu hon nam gioi. Dieu do tao ra kha nang ton tai "gendered spheres of learning" ngay ca khi muc risk tuyet doi thap hon.

Co trien khai duoc o Viet Nam khong?

Co, nhung can dieu chinh cau hoi. Thay vi maternal mortality risk rat cao, Viet Nam co the phu hop hon voi:

  • kien thuc ve bien chung thai ky va sinh mo
  • khoang cach sinh toi uu
  • rui ro mang thai muon
  • chi phi suc khoe tinh than va suc khoe nghe nghiep sau sinh
  • thong tin ve cham soc tre so sinh hoac vaccine

Dieu kien can de lam o Viet Nam

  • Co doi tac clinic, tram y te, benh vien phu san hoac mang luoi cong tac vien dan so.
  • Co kha nang khao sat tach rieng vo va chong ve beliefs, preferred fertility, va bargaining-related outcomes.
  • Co thiet ke intervention du gon de randomize nguoi nhan thong tin trong cap doi.
  • Co cach do outcome hanh vi thuc, khong chi stated preferences.

Tro ngai chinh

  • Muc maternal mortality thap hon khien signal "risk" bot manh va kho lam treatment du sac.
  • Norm ve sinh con o Viet Nam da dang theo vung, dan toc, va cohort, nen external validity se phan manh.
  • Nhieu outcome bargaining kho do hon vi transfer noi ho o Viet Nam thuong khong tach bach thanh qua hay tien mat de quan sat.

Huong mo rong cho Viet Nam

  1. Nghien cuu xem viec cung cap truc tiep thong tin ve rui ro sinh con muon hoac sinh mo cho chong co lam thay doi desired fertility va birth spacing khong.
  2. So sanh hieu qua cua counseling chi cho vo, chi cho chong, va counseling dong thoi cho ca cap trong boi canh kham thai dinh ky.
  3. Mo rong khoi fertility sang child investment: vi du information ve suy dinh duong, vaccine, hay developmental delay co lan giua cha me mot cach doi xung khong.

Mot vai research question moi cho boi canh Viet Nam

  1. Lieu cung cap thong tin cho chong ve chi phi suc khoe the chat va tinh than cua lan sinh mo truoc co lam tang khoang cach sinh o lan ke tiep?
  2. Khi phu nu duoc tu van ve tram cam sau sinh nhung chong khong duoc tu van, thong tin do co thuc su chuyen thanh thay doi trong phan bo thoi gian va ho tro trong ho khong?
  3. Trong cac ho gia dinh co lao dong di cu noi dia, ai la nguoi nam thong tin tot hon ve chi phi cham soc tre nho, va asymmetry do anh huong the nao den quyet dinh sinh them con?

12. Vi sao chon paper nay hom nay

Minh chon paper nay vi no thoa dong thoi bon tieu chi rat manh:

  • la bai top 5 journal economics, cu the la The Review of Economic Studies
  • nam chac trong development economics va micro applied
  • co design thuc nghiem sach, giau bai hoc identification va mechanism
  • co ket qua vua policy-relevant vua conceptually elegant: fertility giam khong phai luc nao cung la cung mot loai outcome

So voi nhieu paper moi khac, bai nay noi bat o cho khong chi co mot treatment effect dep ma con noi rat ro ve cau truc gia dinh dung phia sau effect do.
