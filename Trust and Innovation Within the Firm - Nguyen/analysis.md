## 2. Executive summary

Paper hoi mot cau rat hay va rat "micro" nhung co tam voi mo rong lon: lie u long tin cua CEO doi voi nguoi lam R&D co lam doanh nghiep doi moi hon khong? Nguyen lap luan rang trong moi truong nghien cuu, hop dong va chi so hieu suat khong the mo ta het chat luong va no luc cua nha nghien cuu. Khi ket qua xau co the la do xui ro thay vi nang luc kem, mot CEO co muc trust cao hon se de dung tha thu hon cho that bai va vi vay khuyen khich nhan vien gioi chon cac du an mang tinh tham hiem hon.

De tra loi, paper xay mot bo du lieu matched CEO-firm-patent rat manh, bao phu 5,753 CEO, 3,598 cong ty dai chung My va khoang 700,000 patent trong giai doan 2000-2011. Chien luoc nhan dang chinh co hai lop. Lop thu nhat khai thac thay doi CEO trong cung doanh nghiep de hoi: khi doanh nghiep doi sang CEO co muc trust cao hon, sang tao co tang len khong? Lop thu hai khai thac bien thien within-CEO trong bilateral trust cua cung mot CEO doi voi cac nhom inventor khac nhau, nham tach trust khoi cac manager traits co dinh khac.

Ket qua chinh rat an tuong. Theo abstract cua ban QJE 2026, tang 1 do lech chuan trong CEO trust gan voi 6% patent tuong lai nhieu hon va chat luong patent trung binh cao hon 4-6%. Ban working paper cho thay hieu ung nay den chu yeu tu phia patent chat luong cao, khong phai tu viec "san xuat them patent cho day so luong". Paper con dua bang chung goi y rang CEO trust lam manh hon "trust culture" trong cong ty, va co dau hieu phu hop voi co che khuyen khich exploration/high-risk R&D. Day la mot paper dang doc vi no bien mot khai niem tuong nhu "mem" va kho dinh luong thanh mot bien so co the dua vao design thuc nghiem ban-panel nghiem tuc, dong thoi noi dep giua organization economics, corporate culture va innovation.

## 3. Research question and motivation

### Cau hoi trung tam

Long tin cua CEO doi voi nguoi nghien cuu co lam tang doi moi trong doanh nghiep khong, va neu co thi co che la gi?

### Dong luc hoc thuat

Van lieu ve trust thuong rat manh o cap do vimo va the che: trust lien quan den dau tu, giao dich, phan bo von, tang truong dai han. Nhung khoang trong lon la vi sao trust lai tao ra tang truong ngay ben trong doanh nghiep. Trong khi do, van lieu ve innovation incentives nhan manh vai tro cua tolerance of failure, long-term incentives, monitoring, va organization design. Paper nay chen vao giua hai mach van lieu do bang cach hoi xem trust co the la mot co che to chuc thay the hoac bo sung cho hop dong khong.

### Dong luc thuc tien

R&D la noi bat dinh, kho giam sat, va de co tru phat nham. Neu lanh dao xu ly that bai qua nang tay, nhan su gioi co the tu bo cac du an mang tinh dot pha de quay sang du an an toan. Vi vay, cau hoi cua paper khong chi la "trust co tot khong" ma la trust co giup cong ty chon dung muc rui ro cho sang tao hay khong.

## 4. Main contribution

### Dong gop 1: Dua trust vao manager effects literature mot cach nghiem tuc

Paper bo sung trust nhu mot "manager style" co the quan sat gian tiep va co y nghia kinh te. Day la dong gop dep vi no dua mot net van hoa/niem tin tuong doi "tru tuong" vao khung danh gia CEO traits vo n cung duoc dung cho overconfidence, education, hay corruption culture.

### Dong gop 2: Do trust bang inherited cultural variation va ket hop voi CEO turnovers

Tac gia xay bien CEO trust tu ethnic-origin trust, suy ra tu ho CEO bang cac census giai ma va trust trong GSS. Cac quyet dinh do luong co the bi tranh cai, nhung ve mat sang tao nghien cuu thi rat manh: no cho phep do mot trait sau ben trong khi van co du bien thien de khai thac trong panel firm x year.

### Dong gop 3: Ket hop hai design bo sung nhau

Khong dung lai o within-firm CEO transitions, paper con dung within-CEO bilateral trust doi voi inventors theo quoc gia/ethnic group. Day la diem rat hay. Neu chi co CEO turnovers, nguoi doc co the noi "CEO trust co the chi la proxy cho manager quality". Design within-CEO se cat bot loi phe binh do bang cach giu CEO co dinh, roi hoi tai sao cung mot CEO lai tac dong khac nhau len cac nhom inventor ma minh "tin" nhieu hay it.

### Dong gop 4: Co gang mo khoa co che, khong chi dung o reduced form

Paper khong chi noi trust lam tang patent. No xay model exploration vs exploitation va rut ra du doan rat sac: neu trust khuyen khich risk-taking thi hieu ung se den tu high-quality tail, khong phai tu tang dong loat moi loai patent. Sau do tac gia doi chieu du doan nay voi phan bo chat luong patent.

## 5. Identification or methodology

### Khung ly thuyet

Tac gia xay mot principal-agent model hai ky. Nha nghien cuu co the chon exploration, tuc du an rui ro cao loi nhuan cao, hoac exploitation, tuc con duong an toan hon. CEO khong quan sat truc tiep type va hanh dong, chi quan sat ket qua. Trust duoc model hoa nhu prior belief cua CEO ve viec nha nghien cuu la "good type". CEO trust cao hon se de giai thich that bai la do xui ro thay vi bat tai, nen de dung tha thu hon. Biet dieu do, nha nghien cuu gioi dam chon exploration hon.

### Du lieu

- Patent data tu PATSTAT, bao phu rat rong va cho phep do patent counts, citations, technology class, inventors.
- CEO va top officer data tu BoardEx.
- Ethnic origin suy ra tu last names nhan de-anonymized historical US censuses.
- Generalized trust xay tu cau hoi trust trong General Social Survey, tap trung vao respondents thuoc prestigious occupations de gan hon mau CEO.
- Bilateral trust dua tren Eurobarometer.
- Corporate culture duoc suy ra tu text analysis gan 1 trieu employee reviews tren Glassdoor.

### Design 1: Within-firm CEO turnover

Hoi dong nghia cua design nay la: cung mot firm, khi CEO thay doi tu muc trust thap sang muc trust cao hon, innovation co tang khong? Tac gia dung firm fixed effects, year effects, CEO controls va firm controls. Ban working paper noi ro rang dieu kien nhan dang duoc ho tro boi bang chung cho thay ca timing cua CEO change lan muc trust cua CEO moi deu khong lien quan den pre-change patenting activities. Day khong phai la chuan nhan dang hoan hao, nhung la mot bai kiem tra rat quan trong vi no tan cong truc dien vao lo ngai reverse causality.

### Design 2: Within-CEO bilateral trust

Design thu hai manh hon ve mat cat bo omitted traits. Cung mot CEO, paper so sanh tac dong len cac nhom inventors khac nhau ma CEO co bilateral trust khac nhau doi voi ho. Thong qua CEO fixed effects, firm-year fixed effects, va inventor-country fixed effects, bai viet co gang tach trust khoi nhung dac diem tong quat cua CEO nhu nang luc, charisma, hay quality management.

### Kiem dinh co che qua phan bo chat luong patent

Day la phan rat dep. Paper lap luan rang neu trust chi lam tang no luc hay hop tac theo kieu mean shift thi so patent se tang kha deu o moi muc chat luong. Nguoc lai, neu trust mo khoa exploration/risk-taking thi patent chat luong cao se tang, con patent chat luong thap khong nhat thiet tang. Tac gia dung citation deciles de kiem tra chinh logic nay.

### Diem manh cua phuong phap

- Co hai lop nhan dang bo sung nhau.
- Co noi reduced-form voi co che ro rang.
- Co gang phan biet innovation that su voi xu huong patenting thuan tuy.
- Su dung du lieu lon, matched va co nhieu thong tin phu tro.

### Diem de bi nghi ngo

- Bien trust xay tu ethnic-origin la mot proxy gian tiep; no co the dong thoi gom ca nhung trait van hoa khac.
- CEO turnovers du da kiem tra pre-trends van co the khong hoan toan ngau nhien.
- Ban working paper cho thay mot phan bang chung corporate culture mang tinh suggestive hon la nhan dang nhan qua sach.
- Do innovation bang patents va citations luon co gioi han.

## 6. Results and interpretation

### Ket qua chinh

Theo ban QJE 2026, tang 1 do lech chuan trong CEO generalized trust sau CEO turnover gan voi:

- Khoang 6% patent tuong lai nhieu hon.
- Chat luong patent trung binh cao hon 4-6%.

Trong Table 1 cua working paper, he so baseline cho `CEO's trust` quanh 0.059-0.067 tren bien phu thuoc `asinh(Future patent applications)`, kha on dinh qua nhieu dac ta. Bai viet dien giai 1 do lech chuan trust nhu mot dich chuyen xap xi tu Greek sang English trong thang do inherited trust, giup nguoi doc co intuition ro hon ve quy mo kinh te.

### Ket qua ve bilateral trust

Trong phan mo dau va tom tat ket qua, tac gia bao cao rang ngay ca khi giu CEO co dinh, tang 1 do lech chuan bilateral trust doi voi inventor-country/ethnic group van gan voi 3-5% patent nhieu hon tu nhom tuong ung. Day la bang chung rat co luc vi no noi rang khong phai chi "CEO tot hon" lam tat ca moi thu tot hon, ma dung la muc do tin tuong co su phan bo khac biet theo doi tuong.

### Ket qua ve co che

Day la phan thuyet phuc nhat cua paper. Ban working paper giai thich rang tong so patent khong du de phan biet co che. Khi nhin vao phan bo chat luong patent, trust tang chu yeu o phan duoi cua right tail, nghia la patent chat luong cao tang, con patent chat luong thap khong tang tuong xung. Tac gia dien giai dieu nay la phu hop voi co che trust khuyen khich researchers gioi chap nhan risk va theo duoi exploration, thay vi chi lam viec cham hon hay hop tac hon.

### Ket qua ve corporate culture

Text analysis tu Glassdoor goi y CEO trust di cung corporate trust culture manh hon. Tac gia kha can than o day: ket qua nay "should be taken with great caution" vi mau nho hon va bien van hoa duoc suy ra gian tiep tu reviews online. Dung nhu vay moi thuyet phuc, vi paper khong len gi giong nhu da "chung minh xong" co che, ma dat no dung cho: bang chung ho tro.

### Dien giai kinh te

Y nghia kinh te lon nhat cua paper la trust co the la mot co che governance khi contract khong mo ta het chat luong cong viec sang tao. Trust o day khong phai su "tot bung" mo ho, ma la mot cach xac suat hoa that bai. CEO trust cao hon it quy that bai cua nguoi gioi ve phia "bad type", nen lam giam career risk cua exploration.

## 7. What is special or elegant about the paper

Dieu dep nhat o paper nay la tac gia khong chon loi di de. Neu chi dung reduced-form, paper van da co the rat publishable: CEO trust cao hon, patents nhieu hon. Nhung bai nay co tham vong hon:

- Xay mot model ly thuyet don gian nhung dung mui.
- Rut ra mot du doan co the bac bo duoc ve phan bo chat luong patent.
- Dung them within-CEO bilateral design de tan cong alternative story.

Noi cach khac, day la paper "ghep khoa" rat dep: tu mot khai niem xa xam la trust, tac gia tao ra du lieu, design, va co che du sac de bien cau chuyen thanh mot bai applied micro nghiem tuc.

Mot diem dang hoc nua la cach tac gia dung mot proxy gay tranh cai nhung khong de toan bo bai dung tren no. Vi biet proxy inherited trust se gay hoai nghi, tac gia tu bo sung them bilateral-trust design, robustness voi country-of-origin controls, va bai test phan bo chat luong. Do la phong cach lam nghien cuu rat dang hoc: biet diem yeu cua chinh minh va chu dong xay tam chan de do.

## 8. Required background knowledge

### Trust trong kinh te hoc

Can nam trust o day duoc hieu nhu prior belief ve reliability/type cua nguoi khac, khong chi la "cam giac tot". Cach hieu nay gan voi Gambetta va van lieu economics of trust cua Guiso, Algan-Cahuc, Tabellini.

### Exploration vs exploitation

Day la doi lap kinh dien trong to chuc va innovation. Exploration la tim duong moi, rui ro cao, reward cao; exploitation la toi uu hoa cai dang co, an toan hon. Neu chua quen, co the xem March (1991) va Manso (2011) la hai moc nen.

### Fixed effects va panel identification

Can quen voi y tuong within-firm variation, firm fixed effects, year fixed effects, va vi sao CEO turnovers co the giup tach tac dong cua trait CEO khoi khac biet co san giua doanh nghiep.

### Cultural transmission va inherited traits

Paper dua tren y tuong gia tri van hoa co the duoc truyen qua gia dinh va cong dong di dan. Day khong co nghia tac gia khang dinh "ethnicity la destiny"; no co nghia la ethnic-origin average trust duoc dung nhu mot proxy thong ke cho trait kho do truc tiep o cap CEO.

### Patent quality

Patent counts thuong la thuo c do tho. Citation-weighted patent quality, hay nhin theo citation deciles trong field x year cohort, giup tach patent "co gia tri" khoi patent it anh huong hon. Muon doc ky paper nay thi can comfort voi logic do.

## 9. Limitations and open questions

### Han che 1: Do luong trust rat sang tao nhung cung rat mong manh

CEO trust duoc suy ra tu ho va trust trung binh theo ethnic origin. Day la proxy thong minh nhung co the om ca nhieu thu khac ngoai trust, nhu mot so net van hoa, hoan canh xa hoi-kinh te lich su, hay selection vao cac mang nghe nghiep.

### Han che 2: CEO turnover van khong phai randomization

Tac gia co kiem tra pre-trends va pre-patenting rat can thiet, nhung van con cho de hoi rang board co the chon CEO co trust cao hon dung luc muon doi moi chien luoc R&D. Design within-CEO bilateral trust giam no i lo nay nhieu, nhung khong xoa sach moi lo ngai.

### Han che 3: Patents khong dong nhat voi innovation

Paper co doi dien truc dien lo ngai nay va dua ra them bang chung, nhung nhung doi moi khong patent duoc hoac khong can patent van nam ngoai khung do luong.

### Han che 4: Bang chung ve corporate culture chua that su "clean"

Glassdoor data la goi y manh, khong phai causality sach. Chinh tac gia cung canh bao dieu do.

### Cac cau hoi mo

- Trust co tac dong manh hon trong cac nganh can science frontier so voi nganh can applied engineering khong?
- Trust co tac dong tuong tac the nao voi incentive pay, option compensation, hay project screening systems?
- Trong startup va private firms, noi quy trinh formal yeu hon, hieu ung trust co con manh hon nua khong?
- Co ton tai muc trust toi uu theo loai hinh R&D, dung nhu paper goi y ve "right amount of trust", hay khong?

## 10. Takeaways for a researcher

1. Neu ban muon dua mot khai niem "mem" vao empirical work, hay tim cach buoc no vao mot design co so sanh noi bo that manh. Paper nay khong chi do trust, ma con tim duoc within-firm va within-CEO variation.
2. Mot reduced-form dep hon rat nhieu khi di kem mot co che co the bi bac bo. Test phan bo chat luong patent o day la mau muc cua empirical mechanism design.
3. Khi proxy de bi nghi ngo, dung them mot design khac voi nguon bien thien khac de tao bo bang chung hoi tu.
4. Hay can than voi nguyen nhan va ngon ngu. Phan corporate culture cua paper nay rat hay vi tac gia khong overclaim.
5. Day la vi du tot ve viec noi organization economics voi innovation economics ma khong danh mat tinh applied micro.
6. Neu nghien cuu cua ban dung du lieu lon va proxy phi truyen thong, phan mo ta quy trinh do luong phai rat ro, vi do la noi nguoi doc se tan cong dau tien.

## 11. Vietnam relevance and extension

### Muc do lien quan voi Viet Nam

Paper nay rat lien quan voi Viet Nam, dac biet trong cac cau hoi ve nang suat doanh nghiep, doi moi cong nghe, governance trong firms, va nang luc quan ly cap cao. Viet Nam dang quan tam den upgrading, domestic innovation, FDI spillovers, va kha nang giu chan nhan su ky thuat gioi. Trong moi truong do, bai hoc rang "tolerance of failure co ky luat" co the giup mo khoa R&D la rat dang suy nghi.

### Co the trien khai y tuong nay o Viet Nam khong?

Co the, nhung kho. Se can:

- Du lieu matched quan ly-doanh nghiep-sang tao, co the tu doanh nghiep lon, cong ty cong nghe, hoac du lieu hanh chinh co lien ket sang che, dang ky giai phap huu ich, san pham moi.
- Bien proxy cho managerial trust hoac tolerance of failure. O Viet Nam, last-name-based inherited trust co le rat yeu. Co the thay bang survey truc tiep, psychometric measures, manager fixed-effects tu labor mobility, hoac text/HR records.
- Boi canh co thay doi lanh dao du ro de dung event-study hay movers design.

### Tro ngai chinh

- Du lieu patent o Viet Nam mong hon nhieu so voi My.
- Bien trust kho do sach hon, va co the de lan voi hierarchy, quan he than huu, hay phong cach lanh dao gia truong.
- Internal innovation outputs trong doanh nghiep Viet Nam nhieu khi khong di qua patent ma qua process innovation, supplier upgrading, hoac adaptation.

### Cach cap nhat y tuong cho phu hop hon voi Viet Nam

- Doi patent bang innovation outputs khac: ra mat san pham moi, process change, adoption thanh cong cong nghe moi, quality upgrading, xuat khau san pham co complexity cao hon.
- Doi trust bang tolerance-of-failure practices trong quan ly: cach xu ly project that bai, retention sau that bai, hay quyen tu chu cua team R&D.
- Kiem tra xem manager quality va trust co tac dong khac nhau giua doanh nghiep FDI, private domestic, va SOE hay khong.

### 3 y tuong nghien cuu moi cho boi canh Viet Nam

1. **Managerial tolerance of failure va process innovation trong manufacturing Viet Nam:** Su thay doi giam doc nha may hoac production manager co lam tang adoption cua quy trinh moi, yield improvement, hay defect-reducing experiments khong?
2. **Trust, decentralization, va digital transformation trong doanh nghiep vua va lon:** Nhung doanh nghiep co lanh dao san sang de nhan vien thu nghiem co chuyen doi so nhanh va hieu qua hon khong?
3. **Trust va knowledge transfer trong FDI-domestic supplier linkages:** Cac manager ben mua hang hoac engineering managers co trust cao hon co giup nha cung cap noi dia hoc nhanh hon, nang cap nhanh hon, va giu duoc moi lien ket lau hon khong?

## Danh gia cuoi cung

Day la mot paper rat dang doc trong applied micro ve firms va innovation. No khong phai development paper theo nghia hep, nhung rat co gia tri cho development economics theo nghia nang suat, nang cap cong nghe, va nang luc to chuc. Bai dep o cho no dung mot cau hoi co ve "mem" de tra loi mot van de rat "hard": vi sao mot so to chuc moi doi moi hon to chuc khac. Neu doc de hoc cach lam nghien cuu, bai hoc lon nhat la: mot y tuong tru tuong van co the tro thanh empirical paper rat sac neu ban tim duoc dung bo du lieu, dung variation, va dung mechanism test.
